# Evidence needed for ELVHYS LH2 rupture model comparison

Dataset: Torres de Ritter, Friedrich and Ferraro Porcel de Peralta, ELVHYS 4.3.1
LH2 BLEVE/shock-tube tests, DataverseNO DOI 10.18710/M5LOLM, v1.0, CC0-1.0.
This is a prepared request only; it has not been sent.

To assess a source/model comparison without treating recorder labels as physical
boundary evidence, please provide or identify:

1. For selected date-qualified runs, the exact membrane-rupture trigger event and
   time mapping for each independent recorder, including synchronization offsets
   and uncertainty.
2. Current calibration certificates, dynamic response/bandwidth and zero/baseline
   procedure for the PRESF pressure channels, plus a mapping from every channel to
   its station position and pressure quantity. The requested D4.8 containment
   station drawing is not in the acquired attachment set.
3. Initial tube-side hydrogen/liquid inventory, tube temperature and pressure
   reference basis; oxidizer-run tube composition, humidity and residual gas.
   Initial containment oxygen/temperature must not be assumed to represent tube
   contents.
4. For any thermal comparison, diode-to-apparatus coordinates, event timing, and
   a declared response operator or sensor time constant.
5. For video-supported timing only, a selected clip's clock relation to the
   pressure/temperature recorders and its data-use/redistribution terms.

The existing files support ingestion and source-row access. They do not yet
support a calibrated pressure-wave, fire, LH2 release, or detonation validation.
